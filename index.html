              About

              SongSurf turns any song into a track you ride: every block, turn and burst of speed comes from the music.

              The original

              SongSurf is a fan-made tribute to Audiosurf (2008), created by Dylan Fitterer (Invisible Handlebar). Riding your own music, catching blocks on the beat, chasing combos: the idea is his, and it's still a joy to play.

              Audiosurf on Steam Audiosurf 2

              SongSurf is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Audiosurf or its creator.

              Fair play

              Scores are worked out by the server: it replays your inputs on the same track everyone rides. Runs on your own audio files are practice.

              Built with

              three.js, yt-dlp and FFmpeg. Open source, MIT licensed.
